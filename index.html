A Legacy of Quality

The Story of ZACHS Car Rental

ZACHS Car Rental was established in early 2025 with a clear vision: to revolutionize the car rental landscape in Malaysia by providing a seamless, digitally-driven experience combined with old-school hospitality. Our journey began in Sungai Petani, Kedah, where our founders noticed a significant gap in the market—local travelers were either stuck with expensive multinational corporations or unreliable backyard operators.

We decided to build something different. By focusing on a "Customer-First" philosophy, we invested heavily in a fleet that is not only modern but meticulously maintained. Every vehicle in our fleet undergoes a rigorous 50-point safety inspection before every single rental, ensuring that when you turn the key, you have absolute peace of mind.

Today, ZACHS has grown into a household name for those who value transparency. We pioneered the "No Hidden Fee" guarantee in the region, ensuring that the price you see on your screen is exactly what you pay. No administrative surcharges, no unexpected insurance fees, and no "cleaning" penalties. Our growth is fueled by thousands of returning customers who appreciate our dedication to keeping Malaysia moving.

As we look toward the future, we are expanding our operations to include more electric vehicle (EV) options and enhancing our mobile booking platform to ensure that renting a car is as easy as ordering a meal. We aren't just renting cars; we are enabling adventures, facilitating business growth, and helping families create memories that last a lifetime.

Our Comprehensive Services

More than just a rental company, we are your comprehensive mobility partner in Malaysia.

Short-Term Daily Rentals

Perfect for quick errands, weekend getaways, or visiting family. Our daily rental service offers maximum flexibility with 24-hour rental cycles. We provide instant booking confirmation and a wide variety of vehicles ranging from fuel-efficient hatchbacks to luxury sedans.

Corporate & Business Leasing

We provide tailored solutions for businesses needing long-term mobility without the burden of ownership. Our corporate packages include full maintenance, road tax management, and replacement vehicle guarantees, allowing you to focus on your core business operations.

Airport Meet & Greet

Arriving at KLIA, Penang International, or Alor Setar? Our dedicated team will meet you at the arrival hall. No more waiting in long queues at rental counters. We bring the car directly to the terminal, complete the paperwork in minutes, and get you on your way.

24/7 Emergency Support

Roadside emergencies are unpredictable, but our response isn't. Every ZACHS rental comes with round-the-clock roadside assistance. Whether it's a flat tire, a dead battery, or a mechanical fault, our nationwide network of mechanics is just one phone call away.

One-Way Rentals

Planning a road trip from the North to the South? We offer flexible one-way rental options. Pick up your car in one city and drop it off at any of our designated hubs across Peninsular Malaysia. It's the ultimate solution for travelers seeking a cross-country adventure.

Premium Add-ons

Customize your ride with our range of add-ons. We offer ISOFIX-compliant child seats, GPS navigation units for remote areas, and unlimited mileage packages. Our goal is to ensure that your specific travel needs are met with high-quality equipment.

General FAQ & Rental Terms

Everything you need to know before you hit the road.

All ZACHS rentals include standard Third-Party Liability insurance as mandated by Malaysian law. This covers damage to other people's property and injuries. For your own protection, we highly recommend our Collision Damage Waiver (CDW). This significantly reduces your financial responsibility (excess) in case of damage to the rental vehicle. Please note that insurance does not cover interior damage, lost keys, or fuel contamination.

We provide a 1-hour grace period for returns. Beyond this window, an hourly rate (typically RM15-RM25 depending on vehicle class) will apply. If the return is more than 4 hours late, a full day's rental fee will be charged. We encourage customers to inform us via WhatsApp at least 4 hours in advance if they anticipate a delay to avoid unnecessary penalties and help us manage our fleet schedule.

Most of our standard rentals come with a generous daily limit of 300km, which is sufficient for most travelers in Peninsular Malaysia. For those planning intense cross-country trips, we offer an "Unlimited Mileage" add-on for a small daily fee. Exceeding the standard limit without the add-on will result in a charge of RM0.50 per additional kilometer.

In a post-pandemic world, we take sanitation seriously. Every car undergoes a high-temperature steam cleaning and interior sanitization process after every rental. We pay special attention to "high-touch" areas like the steering wheel, gear shifter, and door handles. If you find the car unsatisfactory at the point of collection, we will replace it immediately or provide a full refund.
