About Us: Empowering Malaysians with Quality Transportation

Founded in 2025, ZACHS Car Rental was born out of a simple yet powerful idea: every Malaysian deserves access to quality transportation without breaking the bank. From our humble beginnings with a small fleet in Sungai Petani, we have expanded nationwide, driven by a commitment to transparency, value, and ease of use.

Recognizing the growing need for flexible mobility solutions—whether for daily commutes, weekend getaways, or business travel—we set out to build a service that prioritizes the customer. We serve thousands of satisfied customers who trust us for their travel needs, ensuring that our cars are always clean, powerful, and ready for the road.

We pride ourselves on having no hidden fees and offering a price match guarantee. Your safety is our priority, which is why we provide comprehensive warranty coverage for mechanical faults and 24/7 roadside assistance.

Terms & Conditions

01. Eligibility

• Minimum age requirement is 20 years old
• Maximum age requirement is 65 years old
• Driving license must be held for a minimum 2 years
• Probationary (P) licences are not accepted

02. Document Required

• Valid driving licence and identity card (IC) for local customers
• Valid International Driving Permit (IDP) and passport for foreign customers
• All documents must be presented in original form and not expired
• Our company reserves the right to reject the rental if documents are incomplete or invalid

03. Payment

• Renter must pay a RM200 security deposit
• Deposit refunded within 7 working days after vehicle return under these conditions:
  ✓ No damage to vehicle
  ✓ Vehicle returned clean
  ✓ Full fuel level as per policy
  ✓ All accessories present
  ✓ No late return charges
• Full formal rental agreement detailing all these terms will be signed upon vehicle delivery and pickup

04. Fuel Policy

• We provide vehicle with FULL fuel level
• Renter must return with SAME fuel level
• Fuel shortfall charged at current market rate + RM20 service fee

05. Rental Duration

• Operating hours: 9:00 am - 8:00 pm
• Document submission verification procedures and signature, and vehicle collection must be done during operating hours
• Rental duration is calculated on a 24-hour basis for daily rental (begin at the time the vehicle is collected)
• Vehicle must be returned at the same time the next day
• Customers are advised to return the vehicle 30 minutes earlier to avoid late charges
• For early return, there is no refund for unused hours
• For late return, the charges will be deducted from the security deposit based on hourly rental rate

06. Traffic Offences & Fines

• Renter bears 100% responsibility for all traffic summonses
• Includes parking fees and fines, toll charges (SmartTAG/Touch 'n Go), speed camera violations, red light camera offenses
• Renter must settle fines within 14 days of notification
• Failure to pay may result in legal action and blacklisting

07. Liability Limitation

• No liability for personal injury or death resulting from your own negligence, actions, or misuse of the vehicle
• No liability for loss of personal belongings due to your own failure to secure them properly
• No liability for any consequential losses (financial, travel delays, missed appointments) caused by your own errors or decisions

08. Vehicle Use Restrictions

We STRICTLY PROHIBITED:
• Smoking inside vehicle, transporting pets without carrier, or carrying durian/food with strong odors (result in forfeiture of the full deposit as cleaning fee)
• Off-road driving, sub-renting to third parties, commercial passenger transport, or towing other vehicles (result in immediate rental termination)
• Driving under influence of alcohol/drugs, racing or speed testing, or engaging in illegal activities (result in immediate termination of rental, police notification, and legal prosecution)
• Geographical Restrictions: Operating vehicle outside Peninsula Malaysia, entering restricted/closed areas, attempting border crossing (result in immediate rental termination)

09. Rental Termination

Upon violation, we will:
• Remotely immobilize the vehicle via GPS tracking
• Recover it via company-controlled towing
• Retain all deposits as recovery and administrative fees

10. Damage, Loss & Theft

• Renter responsible for full cost of repairs for damages (interior and exterior damage)
• Loss of vehicle accessories (conditions will be checked after returning vehicle)
• Theft of vehicle due to unsecured keys (GPS tracking activated, full deposit forfeiture (RM200), and potential additional recovery charges)

Reporting Procedure:
